Bring your count matrix

Upload CSV or TSV with RNA identifiers and raw, nonnegative integer counts. Sample columns must start with Control_, AD_, or PSP_. At least two samples per comparison group are required.

Uploaded counts use library-size CPM normalization and log₂(mean case CPM + 1) − log₂(mean control CPM + 1). No statistical significance is inferred.

COLIBRI-AD / METHODOLOGY

A workspace for exploration.

Comparative Transcriptomic Profiling & Molecular Biomarker Mapping for Alzheimer’s Disease and Tauopathy Stratification.

The default cohort, expression values, p-values and pathway associations are synthetic demonstration data. Uploaded raw counts are normalized to counts per million (CPM), then summarized by group. Fold changes use a +1 pseudocount. Differential-expression significance testing is not implemented.

The brain graphic is a schematic, not a clinical image or segmentation. Manual MRPI calculations and the weighting sandbox are exploratory. No trained XGBoost/LightGBM model, EPAD risk index, or diagnostic output is included.

Raw-read pipelines, scan processing, statistically validated differential analysis, target enrichment and model validation require backend integrations.
